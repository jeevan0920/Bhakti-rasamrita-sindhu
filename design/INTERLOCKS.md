## §1 · Why this file exists

You left four documents and one fault: each page is honest, and the four are not one corpus. The same fact is stated twice with two different answers, and a draftsman reads whichever page he finds last. So the seven invariants are printed here once, with the address of every place they appear, and a reviewer who finds a page disagreeing with this one has found the page to return. This file adds no rule. It records the rules you have already ruled.

Two answers to one fact is not a difference of opinion. It is two houses sharing a wall, and a draftsman outside cannot tell which one he is entering.

**The third return, and what it changed.** The corpus came back because the same fact stood twice with two answers. It has since been repaired by two hands against the five invariants that then stood, and the repairs drifted where neither hand could see the other: the night had no second rung, so every night sentence that was not primary was set in the faint ink; the door had no night ink; the foot's measure was borrowed from a bell's ceiling; and the margin's *only* had never counted the ordinal it holds. Those four were corrected at the points below, and after that pass every *still says* in §2 was gone, because there was nothing left for it to say.

**The fourth return, and what it added.** It brought one thing that was not a fault and not an argument: a debt. At 300% a nine-letter word is 414 points in a 294-point column, the corpus had refused the three answers that were available, and a page that names a fault and then leaves it is a page that has learned to live with it. The answer is the verse pane — the block may be moved sideways because the verse was the text that may not be broken — and it was the sixth invariant. The fifth return found the second surface in the journal's field. The one rule that did move is 01 §4.4's prohibition, and it moved in the page where it was written, under D22, with its exception printed beside it; this file added the sixth invariant and did not soften the fifth to make room for it, which is §5's rule and not an exception to it.

**The fifth return, and the one-way rule.** Eight screens came back. In every return part of the fault was a page that owned a rule and still printed the old one, and on Day 48 you said the whole of it: each page is honest and the pages are not one corpus. The eleven screens are now the authority for their own claims. Where a sealed screen contradicts a foundation page, the foundation page moves, and it moves only at the place named; no screen moves from a page. This pass is that rule carried out. I1 now places the seal's square at the foot of the block at 300%, horizontal with the locator and the ordinal, the 24 × 24 that S-09 §5 [A] drew and that 01 §3.1, 02 §11 and 04 A5 give it; the open question is answered and the word *open* leaves the invariant.

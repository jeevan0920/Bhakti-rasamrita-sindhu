# OPEN BEFORE THE BUILD
## The gate page: what he signed, and what he left owing

He sealed eleven screens. He also wrote, in three of those seals, that certain things were **owed before a build and not before the seal** — and he asked for two sittings he did not get. This page is that list, in one place, so that nothing he marked owed is lost inside a document of four hundred lines.

It is not a second ledger. `APPROVED-DESIGNS.md` records the seals; this records the debts that stand between the seals and a build.

**Status, Day 54.** Section C is closed: the six duplicated facts now print one answer each. Section A is not: the three arithmetic corrections below are still open, and the work on them was stopped rather than hurried. Section B cannot be closed by anyone in this repository.

## B. The two sittings he demanded and did not get

He used *Bring me the person* twice, which is the seal his own team fears most. Both are recorded with the person and the question. Neither has happened.

**Sitting one — the stranger.** A person who has never heard of the book, in a language he does not read. He is to be given the phone with S-01 open and nothing else, and watched for the first two seconds, before he touches anything. The question: does he find one act without a sign. What is recorded: what he does first, whether he asks what it is, and whether he leaves without acting.

**Sitting two — Kamalā Devī, seventy-one, at 04:40.** She keeps sixteen rounds with the screen off. The question: where is the string when she keeps them — whether the press is a bead, or only the phone keeping her place. What is recorded: which hand, where the phone lies, how many rounds she completes without looking, and whether the count she sees afterwards is welcome or a debt.

Neither sitting can be done by a designer, an agent, or a document. They are the two places where the corpus stops and a person has to be met. Until they happen, S-01 and S-03 carry seals that rest on a stranger and a grandmother who have not been in the room.

## 0. The act

The act is none, and this is the only one of the eleven of which that is true. Nothing is done on this surface that was not already being done when the page broke. His hand does one of two things. It presses the act that did not go through, in the same 158 × 88 at x 160–318, under the same word — *Hear it*, *Keep*, *Open the reader* — and the page tries once more. **The hour's state of mind.** A person meets the floor at the moment a thing he wanted did not happen, which is a small anger or a small fear, and at 03:40 a large one. The surface is built for the tired reading and not for the calm one: three short sentences, a verb that has not changed, and a door where the door always is. ## 9. The five gates

**Pramāṇa — is it true.** The floor prints no sentence of the book, so this gate is answered about the device and not about the text. Every sentence here is a fact with a tense: what did not finish is past, what is still true is present, and what will happen is a mechanism and not a promise — *when the phone finds a network*, never *we will try again*. A build that prints one of these sentences for a thing it is not holding has lied, and that is the floor's only doctrinal fault: it is a claim about where a man's words are. The six destroyers are the other half of this gate, because a sentence that adds a feeling to a fact is a false sentence about the app's own state (03 §1.4, second rule).

**Auciya — is it fitting.** To this person, this hour, this state. At 04:00 the failure is not on the glass; at 03:40 the floor does not come; at 14:20 on 2G it is three sentences and no more; at first open it is nothing at all. What is not fitting and is refused: a sheet over an ārati, a question at the hour of loss, an ask at the door, and the three sentences of a page he left at midnight arriving at 04:20 as though the hour had not changed (03 §5.5: a true thing at the wrong moment is a false thing in effect).

**Sthāyī — does it deepen the permanent mood.** The floor has no rasa of its own and must not pretend to one. Its danger is the passing wave: the small anger of a thing that did not work, the small relief of a thing that did. Its service is that a failure changes nothing about the practice — the ring keeps its marks, the bead counts, the name needs nothing, and the page he came for is still the page he came for. The test is the deletion test: remove the block and he would not know where his note is, so the block is load-bearing; remove the sympathy and nothing is lost, because there was none to remove.

**Ābhāsa — does it risk a counterfeit.** Four. The apology with a personality is the shape of care without its substance, and `BRS 4.9` calls the imitation worse than none because it trains the palate to accept the false. The network named as a condition of the app is a claim about our engineering dressed as a fact about his phone. The empty state that sells — a sample, a tour, *see how it works* — is `prajalpa` with a button on it. And the failure that performs its own honesty, *we could not reach our servers and we are working on it*, is a sentence about us at the one moment he needs a sentence about his note.

**Adhikāra — is this person qualified to receive it.** Nothing here is gated and nothing here gates. The one risk on this surface is leakage: a failure that named a verse, a person, a room's words or a depth would hand to whoever is holding the phone something the gate exists to keep, which is why the floor prints none of them and why the lock screen never carries it. And the surface is not for the advanced: at bhāva and prema nothing changes here, because the floor is the same floor for the man who has practised thirty years and the man who arrived on Tuesday.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

1. **The sentence test.** Every failure string is exactly three sentences and at most 42 words, in the order past, present, future; no code, no apology, no question mark, no exclamation mark, no semicolon, no parenthesis, no colon outside a time. A build whose string is four sentences, or two, does not produce an artefact.
2. **The first-open test.** Cold install, no network, open and press nothing for ninety seconds. No platform sheet has been raised, no pre-line is on the glass, no sentence of §8 is on the glass — `empty.once` belongs to the day's floor from the second open and not before — and the screen behind any sheet that the platform raises by itself is the platform's own, not ours.
3. **The offline test — the one that must fail if this surface is wrong.** Put the handset in a drawer for thirty days with the radio off, then open it, then open the room, then open the journal. If any string on the glass names the network, the signal, the connection or a wait outside a failure's third sentence; if a spinner, a dot, a badge, a bar or a sheet is drawn; if any count appears — the surface is returned. The pass is a screen that says nothing, and the first build will fail it inside the hour, because a build wants to announce that it is offline. This is the test the whole of §5 [A] and §6 exists for, and it is the one a build fails by being helpful.
4. **The 04:00 test.** With a recording held and the clock at 04:20, the day names maṅgala and 04:20, the sheet does not rise, and the recording is not mentioned until the journal is opened.
5. **The word test.** Set 300% on the 320 frame, open every string of §8 in its own state — `S11.label.name.janmastami` among them, in [C] — and photograph the block. No word is broken, no line is shrunk, no hyphen is added, no ellipsis appears, no run in the block measures more than 272 at 54, and no name in [C] measures more than 224 at its capped size: *recitation*, ten narrow letters, is 270 and stands, a ten-letter run carrying one *m* is 289 and returns the string, and *Janmāṣṭamī* at 60 is 342, which no column on the surface holds; it stands at 36, which is 205, and that is 04 A15's number.

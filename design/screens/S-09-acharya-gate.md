## 13. The one contest

**I contest objection 2, and only its second clause** — the work's name as the chain's second line. The first clause is done and is in the body: the heading's second sentence is 42 characters with the space, three lines of a 294-point column, 84 points where the page had 28, and the block ends at 479.

**What he is right about.** At 100% the name fits, and his count is exact: 22 glyphs at 9 points is 198 in 294, one line of 30, and 423 + 56 + 30 = 509 with 57 points of air left. The ear is given *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* whole at stop 3 of §8 and the eye is given a column, and a page whose subject is the address should not be the one page that withholds a name.

**What his sentence does not carry.** The page must also stand at 300% on the 320. There the chain is at 54, a glyph is 27, and the column is 224: the whole name is 22 × 27 = 594, and the piece that would have to stand whole — *rasāmṛta-*, the longest of the three the hyphens allow, 7, 9 and 7 glyphs — is 9 × 27 = 243. That is nineteen points past the column. It is not a judgement about the name; it is 243 against 224. And a 22-glyph compound with two hyphens can be broken at those hyphens or not at all, so 243 is the least any line of it can be. I can find three ways to print it: break a word (`01` §2.3 rule 7, and the Day 49 return was about a word that would not fit); set the line, or the chain, below the size the reader has chosen (`04` §4.4: *no line is ever made smaller to make a verse fit*); or write the line short, which is what this page does. The third is a loss and I am not dressing it as a gain: §7 of his review is right that the eye is given a column, and a column is not a name.

**If he rules against me.** The name goes in as the chain's second line in every state and at every size: one line of 30 at 100%, two of 120 at 200%, three of 270 at 300% on the 390, and at 300% on the 320 the chain's whole block takes the rung below — 36/60, the rung the foot line already takes on that phone, where *rasāmṛta-* is 9 × 18 = 162 of 224 and no word breaks. So the name stays out, §11's row stands as it was written, and the nineteen points are the whole of the reason.

## 1. The aṅga and the source

**No limb. The threshold's authority is Seal VI.** The three the register's earlier line gave this screen (R-01 · Round 2 · 2) — 43, the hearing (BRS 1.2.74–95; 1.2.238); 42, the ārati (BRS 1.2.87); 63, the name (BRS 1.2.149–150; 04 §6.1) — are the reader's, the ārati's and the name's, and not this screen's: the hearing in the reader, on the screen where the reader is; the ārati at its hour; the name at every junction with no act. Nothing is done here but the reading, and a reading is not a limb. The register's row for S-01, 02 §19 and 03 §10.3 are not this sheet's to state, and the claims this sheet made about them are struck (§12): a page may not say of another page that it is mended.

**The source, and the verse.** The corpus gives **BRS 1.2.238** as the source of the aṅgas: the five potent forms, and the sentence that a small attachment to any one of them can arouse devotion even in a neophyte. That verse's Sanskrit is not printed here. The brief §7 permits the Sanskrit of 1.1.11 and of four other places, and does not permit the five potent forms' verse; the threshold's verse must be one the corpus can stand behind, and 1.2.238 stays in this document as the source of the limbs and not as a string on the screen. The verse the stranger reads is **BRS 1.1.11**, the definition the five forms serve. Its rendering is the one sentence 01 §2.4's specimen carries, because one verse may not carry two renderings in one corpus. I have read that specimen this morning and not taken it from a log: 01 §2.4 prints the corrected sentence and its drawing sets it in nine lines at 30/42, 402, in the same 634 as before, and the log at 01 §16 records the same. It is a rendering and not a translation, and the chain says so.

**The reviewer.** No person has signed. The chain at the foot reads `BRS 1.1.11 · reviewer unnamed · translator unnamed` — 50 characters, the unsigned form of I2 — and the seal's place in the margin is 18 × 18 of nothing. The mark's first printed appearance in this corpus will be a sentence a person signed, and it is not this one.

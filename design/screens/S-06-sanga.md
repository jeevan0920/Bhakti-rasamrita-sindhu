## 9. The five gates

**I. Pramāṇa.** The five limbs are in §1 with their locator, and the one verse that makes this a surface and not a corner is `BRS 1.2.238`, where saṅga is named among the five potent forms. **II. Auciya.** The five bodies are in §2 and each one is answered by a measurement, not by a manner. **III. Sthāyī.** What is stronger after this than before is a name he can say. The specification's own test for this surface is *members who can name a person, not a post* (spec §10), and the screen is built so that the only things on it that are persons are a name at the head and a name above a word. The bow returns nothing, so the practice is not a wage, and the room forgets, so the practice is not a record to defend. That is the whole of the deepening and it is deliberately small: a man who has been bowed to once, by a room he can name, has something the next morning that a like cannot give him, and the app did not have to tell him it happened.

| The counterfeit | What refuses it |

**V. Adhikāra.** No one under thirteen outside family mode, and the register says it in one line.
